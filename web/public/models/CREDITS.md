# 3D heart models

Both are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They were
compressed for the web (WebP textures at 2048 px, meshopt geometry; the interior model simplified to
100,000 triangles) with glTF-Transform 4.5.1. Otherwise unchanged.

| File | Used for | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| `heart-realistic.glb` | The landing page, and the outside of the heart in Learn | This work is based on "[Realistic Human Heart](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/realistic-human-heart-3f8072336ce94d18b3d0d055a1ece089)" by [neshallads](https://sketchfab.com/neshallads), licensed under CC BY 4.0 |
| `heart-interior.glb` | The inside of the heart (chambers, valves, septum) in Learn | This work is based on "[Human Heart Internal Structure 3D Model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/human-heart-internal-structure-3d-model-21d346f72230432e8ed5fe448b03cca5)" by [Haiqa Arif](https://sketchfab.com/ansarihaiqaarif), licensed under CC BY 4.0 |
